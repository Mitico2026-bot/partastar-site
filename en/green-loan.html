Frequently asked questions

Is the green loan the same at every bank?

Every bank has its own terms and requirements. First check the requirements of the bank you plan to borrow from. We will provide technical information that meets those requirements.

What documents does the bank need?

Usually the supplier's quote, the product's technical specifications and certificates. Ask your bank for the exact list.

Which products can be bought on a loan?

You can choose any component of a solar system from the catalog: solar panels, inverters and energy storage batteries. Check in advance whether it is on the bank's list.

How do I get a quote?

Write the name of the product you are interested in on Messenger.
