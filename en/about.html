About us

Partastar Solar: the official site offering Partastar products in Mongolia.

Partastar

Partastar's marketing center is located in Shenzhen. The brand was founded in 2023 and has 16 years of experience in the battery industry. It has its own cell factory, 3 assembly plants, 15 subsidiaries in China and 6 branches abroad.

The products carry ROHS, CE, UN38.3, MSDS, IEC and other international certifications. 43 international certificates and 26 patents have been obtained. Sold in more than 64 countries and regions including the USA, Canada, Europe, South Korea, Australia, the Middle East and Africa.

Frequently asked questions

Will the battery work in winter cold?

The temperature range differs by model. For example, most models charge at 0~60℃ and discharge at −20~60℃. The Stack IP65 series charges at −10~55℃. We will advise on a suitable model and installation location for winter conditions.

How many years will the battery last?

The catalog states 6000+ cycles. Actual service life depends on usage and temperature.
