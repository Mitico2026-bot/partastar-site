## 8. Green loan and FAQ

**Green loan process:** 1) choose a model; 2) get a quote on Messenger;
3) submit the quote, technical specifications and certificates to the bank;
4) after approval we supply and install.

- *Is the green loan the same at every bank?* No — each bank has its own terms.
  Check your bank's requirements; we provide matching technical information.
- *What documents does the bank need?* Usually the supplier quote, technical
  specifications and certificates. Ask your bank for the exact list.
- *Which products can be bought on a loan?* Any solar-system component in the
  catalog (panels, inverters, storage batteries). Check the bank's list first.
- *Will the battery work in winter cold?* Depends on model. Most charge at
  0~60℃ and discharge at −20~60℃; Stack IP65 charges from −10℃. We advise on a
  suitable model and installation location for winter.
- *How many years will the battery last?* The catalog states 6000+ cycles;
  actual life depends on usage and temperature.
- *How much does it cost?* We quote each system individually — write on Messenger.
